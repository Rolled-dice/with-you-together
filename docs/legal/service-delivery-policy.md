## Digital service

SomeoneNear provides online companionship and everyday-support services. We do not ship physical products. There are no physical shipping charges or postal delivery times for a SomeoneNear membership.

Only the services and benefits shown with your purchase are included. Availability can differ between India and the United States, by telephone coverage, and by membership.

## Membership access

Membership access is activated after payment confirmation is received and matched to your account. Expected activation time: [ACTIVATION_TIME]. Sign in using the account associated with the purchase to access the benefits.

If you have been charged but access is missing, contact [VERIFIED_SUPPORT_EMAIL] with your account email, transaction reference and approximate payment time. Do not send your password, one-time code, full card number or bank credentials. We will investigate the payment status before asking you to pay again.

## Listener calls

Request a call at least two hours in advance. A request is subject to listener availability and becomes confirmed when you receive confirmation. The confirmation identifies the agreed date, time, timezone and any included duration.

Calls are delivered to the supplied telephone number. Keep the number correct and available at the booked time. Supported destinations: [SUPPORTED_CALLING_REGIONS]. A plan does not guarantee calling coverage for every number in a country.

The completed Cancellation & Refund Policy explains the treatment of rescheduling, missed calls, technical failures and cancellations.

## Companion and reminders

AI Companion access depends on service availability, connectivity, safety checks and the membership limits shown at purchase. AI responses are automated and are not a promise of human review or emergency assistance.

Reminders depend on the schedules you confirm, your timezone, and delivery through the selected app, email, calendar or messaging channel. Calendar services may require you to accept or import an invitation. Sending an invitation does not prove that it appeared in your calendar or that you acted on it.

SomeoneNear cannot guarantee exact notification timing or uninterrupted delivery. Use another method for essential medication management. Prescription extraction and reminders are not clinical verification.

## Scheduled messages and communications

Scheduled messages are delivered through the channel and recipient selection shown in the feature. Check the intended recipient, date and timezone. Delivery may depend on the recipient's account, network and provider settings. WhatsApp or SMS availability will be stated when the relevant channel is enabled.
