## Browser storage

SomeoneNear uses cookies and similar technologies, including local storage and session storage, to maintain sign-in, remember relevant settings, secure the service and record privacy choices. These technologies can contain identifiers associated with an account or device.

Essential storage supports services you request. Blocking it through your browser may prevent sign-in or other account functions. Optional analytics and marketing technologies, if introduced, require the choices and permissions applicable to your location.

## Your choices

Use Manage Privacy Choices to review and change available optional categories. You may also clear stored data or block cookies in your browser. Clearing storage may sign you out and require you to set your choices again.

Refusing optional tracking does not authorize tracking by another route. Where applicable law requires recognition of an opt-out preference signal, SomeoneNear must apply it to the relevant processing. [VERIFY_SIGNAL_HANDLING_IF_APPLICABLE.]

Cookie choices do not authorize medication sharing, AI memory, call recording or marketing messages. Those uses need their own notices and permissions where applicable.
