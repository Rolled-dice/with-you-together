## Health information we collect and why

SomeoneNear, operated by [LEGAL_PERSON_OR_ENTITY_NAME], collects the following information when you use the corresponding service. The categories include information that may identify your physical or mental health or allow health-related inferences.

| Category | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Medication names, dosages, instructions, prescriptions, schedules and reported dose actions | Organize the medication information you supply, extract text and deliver requested reminders |
| Mood entries, journal content and wellbeing concerns | Provide check-in and journal features |
| Health-related content voluntarily included in Companion conversations, memories or summaries | Generate responses and provide continuity |
| Safety classifications concerning crisis or self-harm signals | Apply safety boundaries and handle relevant safety concerns |
| Health-related call-booking information and necessary listener notes | Arrange and deliver the requested conversation and address an issue |
| Identifiers, contact details and family relationships linked to these records | Associate information with the correct person and carry out authorized delivery or sharing |

Sources include you, a family member or representative acting with authority, listeners who provide a requested service, and inferences or summaries produced from your interactions. A medication or wellbeing entry can be health information even if SomeoneNear is not a medical service.
