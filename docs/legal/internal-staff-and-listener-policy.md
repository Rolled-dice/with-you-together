# SomeoneNear internal staff and listener policy

Prepared 1 October 2026. Proposed internal operating policy. Owner: [POLICY_OWNER]. Effective date: [INTERNAL_EFFECTIVE_DATE]. Adoption, training and technical enforcement remain to be completed. This is not a substitute for an employment or contractor agreement.

## Access and confidentiality

Use personal staff accounts with the approved authentication controls. Access only records needed for an assigned task. A technical permission to view a record is not a reason to open it. Never share credentials or use another person's session.

Treat conversations, prescriptions, medication records, moods, family details, call content and recordings as confidential. Do not copy them into personal email, messaging apps, unapproved AI tools or private storage. Do not take screenshots or download records without a documented operational need and approved storage.

Do not give private chats or health information to a payer or family owner solely because of that role. Verify the person's identity, the relevant authority, and the permitted scope of disclosure. Requests for another adult's information must be handled through the approved privacy process.

## Listener scope

Listeners provide companionship and everyday conversation. Do not claim to be a clinician acting through SomeoneNear, diagnose conditions, interpret prescriptions, recommend changing medication, or guarantee a person's safety. State the limits of the role when a request falls outside it.

Do not solicit money, gifts, loans, personal relationships or contact outside approved service channels. Do not exploit dependence or pressure a member to keep using the service. Report threats, harassment or a boundary concern through [STAFF_ESCALATION_CHANNEL].

## Calls and notes

Confirm the intended participant without disclosing sensitive information to whoever answers the telephone. Explain who you are and the purpose of the call. Respect a refusal to continue.

Keep notes factual, relevant and limited to the service. Distinguish what the member said from your own observation. Do not add diagnoses, speculation about medication use, unnecessary intimate details or judgments about relatives. A call's completion does not prove the member is safe or medically well.

Do not record with a personal device. Service recording must remain off unless the approved purpose, participant permissions, access restrictions and retention process are all in place. Stop recording when required following withdrawal and refer questions about existing recordings to the privacy owner.

## Safety concerns

If a member describes imminent danger, encourage contact with local emergency services and follow the approved location-specific escalation procedure. Do not promise a rescue, immediate response or monitoring that SomeoneNear cannot provide.

Escalate through [SAFETY_LEAD_AND_CONTACT]. Share only information necessary for the approved action and legally permitted purpose. Record the concern, steps taken, person notified and relevant times without copying an entire transcript unnecessarily. Do not automatically notify every family member; the appropriateness, authority and safety implications must be considered.

Before listener operations begin, approve [INDIA_SAFETY_PLAYBOOK] and [US_SAFETY_PLAYBOOK], staff coverage, emergency-resource references and escalation authority. AI detection is not a replacement for a human escalation process or an assurance that all risks will be recognized.

## Consent and requests

Do not tick consent boxes for a user, backdate acceptance or describe an unverified permission as verified. Document the notice version and the actual choice through the approved process. If a person withdraws a permission, route it promptly and stop the relevant optional use as required.

Forward access, correction, deletion, guardian and representative requests to [PRIVACY_OWNER]. Use proportionate verification. Do not ask for a government identity document by default or send one through an ordinary support channel unnecessarily.

Do not mark deletion complete until the relevant systems and providers have been checked. The scope may include Supabase records and uploads, AWS summaries and audit events, email/calendar deliveries, telephony records, messaging providers and lawful backup handling. Record exceptions and explain them through the approved response.

## Payments and complaints

Use the approved purchase and cancellation terms. Never request a password, card security code, bank login or one-time payment code. Do not promise a refund amount or deadline while the refund policy remains undecided. Preserve any statutory right and route billing complaints to [BILLING_OWNER].

Record a complaint's receipt, owner, applicable deadline and outcome. Explain any delay or required information. Staff must not obstruct a cancellation or pressure a user to withdraw a complaint.

## Security incidents

Immediately report misdirected reminders, unauthorized access, exposed recordings, lost devices, incorrect family disclosures or unexpected provider data use to [SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY_INCIDENT_CONTACT]. Preserve relevant evidence in approved storage. Do not erase logs or conceal the incident.

The incident lead must contain the issue, assess affected people and information, involve relevant providers and legal counsel, and determine notification duties in the applicable markets. Staff must not invent a universal notification deadline or notify affected people using an unapproved account.

## Retention, providers and offboarding

Follow the approved retention schedule. Do not retain sensitive records indefinitely for possible future use. Apply documented legal holds only to the necessary records and review their expiry.

Do not activate a new AI, analytics, recording, email or payment provider without checking its data access, contractual role, retention, security, processing locations and the required user notice. An existing policy naming a category of provider is not authorization for every new use.

Remove access promptly when a staff assignment or engagement ends. Recover approved equipment and storage, and record completion. Confidentiality obligations continue after access ends, subject to applicable law and the relevant agreement.
