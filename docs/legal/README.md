## Completing the package

Square-bracketed uppercase fields are unresolved details. They are not intended to appear on the public website. Fill them consistently across documents. Blank refund cells must remain blank until the owner provides the rules.

The preparation date is not the effective date. Assign one approved policy version and effective date when the completed texts and corresponding product behaviour are ready. Record the version users actually see and accept.

The consent wording and internal policy describe proposed requirements. They do not establish that the software already enforces them. Review notes identify gaps found in the current checkout; production settings and provider agreements were not audited.

Before publication, complete the operating entity/address, working support contacts, age policy, retention schedule, service activation expectations, cancellation process and refund rules. Verify the actual payment provider and processor arrangements. Obtain legal review for the chosen India and US launch scope, particularly health information and young users.

Razorpay onboarding or approval is not guaranteed by these documents. The unfinished refund worksheet must be completed before it is used as a published refund policy or submitted as a completed policy for provider review.
